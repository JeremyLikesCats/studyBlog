## Coulomb's law
Coulomb's law states the following: 

For two charges $q_1$ and $q_2$, the following is true:

:::note
*Coulomb's law*
$$
\vec{F}_2 = \frac{1}{4\pi \epsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2 \hat{r}_{21}}{r_{21}^2}
$$
:::
